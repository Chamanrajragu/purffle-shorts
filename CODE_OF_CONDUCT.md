# Code of conduct

PurffleShorts follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the code and not the person, and make it easy for newcomers to ask questions. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Email **info@purffle.com** if someone breaks these rules. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may ban people who break them repeatedly or seriously.
